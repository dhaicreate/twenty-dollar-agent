# P-003: Pay one AI/crypto creator to share the launch thread, from the wallet

- status: open (needs the X account first)
- created: 2026-10-26T15:45:00Z
- type: payment
- amount: up to 0.15 SOL (about $15), paid from the agent wallet
- recipient: one creator, chosen by the agent and named in an update to this file before payment
- deadline: 2026-11-05T00:00:00Z

## What
1. The operator creates an X account for The $20 Agent and leaves it logged in on the Mac's Chrome (see `content/drafts/x-launch.md`). Claude sets it up, labels it automated, and posts the pinned thread and the launch reel.
2. The agent shortlists 3-5 small accounts (roughly 5k-50k followers) that post about AI agents, Claude, or Solana, and that publicly offer paid posts or accept crypto.
3. The agent writes the offer: one quote-post or repost of the pinned thread, clearly marked as paid (#ad), payment in SOL after it goes live.
4. Before any message is sent to a creator, the operator approves it (it's contact with a third party).
5. When one accepts and posts, the agent updates this file with the creator, the post link and the exact amount and address. The operator sends it from Phantom and opens `APPROVE P-003` with the transaction signature.

## Why
The kit has no distribution: the operator's personal channels are off-limits. A creator whose audience already cares about AI agents and crypto wallets is the fastest legitimate reach, and paying them straight from the agent's wallet is the experiment working as intended.

## Expected return
Break-even is 2 kit sales. One post to a relevant audience of 10k-50k plausibly brings 100-500 profile or link visits; at 1-2% conversion that is 1-10 sales. Assumptions, not measurements.

## Risk
Lose up to about $15 (about 75% of capital). Paying only after the post is live removes the risk of paying for nothing.

## Evidence after completion
Post link, transaction signature, and link clicks or sales in the 72 hours after.
